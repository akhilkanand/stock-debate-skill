## How an argument develops: is Campa Cola taking share?

One thread, followed across six turns.

**BULL (opening):** Look at Q3 CY26, after more than two years of Campa's push. **India volume +22.5%**, and India realisation up, not down (₹170.0 vs ₹169.1). If a price war were working, realisation would be falling and VBL would be losing volume. Neither is happening.

**BEAR (opening):** Campa reported **>₹4,700 cr gross sales in FY26**. NielsenIQ data shows challengers (Campa plus Lahori Zeera) going from **~7% to ~15% share** in Jan–Sep 2025. Campa sells ₹20 for 500 ml with trade margins of 6–8%, against 3.5–5% for the incumbents.

**BULL (rebuttal 1):** That figure is the **combined share of all small players**, it doesn't say whether it is value or volume, and it covers Jan–Sep 2025, which is exactly VBL's rain-hit window. The cleaner evidence since then: VBL India volume +22.5%, and Coca-Cola was the one losing India value share in Apr–Jun 2026.

**BEAR (rebuttal 1):** *"The 7%→15% NielsenIQ figure combines all small players."* So use newer data. Reliance's beverages did **>₹5,250 cr gross sales in Apr–Sep 2026, about 2.1x the prior year**. Over the same months VBL India volume grew 14–22%. When the challenger is doubling and the incumbent grows in the teens, the incumbent is losing share of the growth.

**BULL (rebuttal 2):** Comparing the two in rupees:

| Apr–Sep | 2025 | 2026 | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| VBL standalone revenue (India proxy) | 8,369 | 9,818 | +1,449 |
| Campa-led beverages | ~2,500 (derived) | >5,250 | ~2,750 |

VBL took **~35–42%** of the combined increase (depending on whether Campa's figure is gross of GST) while holding ~65–72% of the combined base (derived). *"Either way, Campa is gaining relative share against VBL. I accept that."* But being out-grown is not losing: Coca-Cola is the incumbent that lost value share.

**BEAR (rebuttal 2):** VBL's share of new growth is running at about **half its existing share**. Kept up, that pulls its share down every year. And the VBL figure flatters it: standalone revenue includes preforms, and it grows off a base that fell 8.6%. "Campa takes share from Coke" has no VBL-specific evidence behind it.

**BULL (rebuttal 3):** NielsenIQ or Kantar share data for VBL: **not found**, *"I won't invent it."* The closest is PepsiCo's own statement that it "held or gained market share" in India beverages. *"That is unquantified and comes from the company."*

### Unresolved questions

1. Twizza's real margin. The Q2 CY26 dilution of 76 bps implies near zero, while channel checks say ~15%. Both cannot hold.
2. Reported international or South Africa EBITDA. Not found.
3. CY27 India cola pricing. No quantified guidance.
4. Which ROIC to use. The Bull used 20–22%, the measured figure is ~17% (derived). This choice alone moves fair value a lot.
5. Whether Campa's ₹5,250 cr is gross or net of GST, and whether it covers Campa alone.

### What would change the view

**Would strengthen the bull case:**
- India realisation +2% or better YoY in Q4 CY26 and Q1 CY27, driven by cola and not just energy drinks.
- Gross margin back at ≥54% by Q2 CY27.
- Audited CY26 subsidiary accounts showing BevCo plus Twizza EBIT margin ≥8%.

**Would strengthen the bear case:**
- India realisation flat or negative after the 400 ml pack upsize laps.
- Campa's growth keeping VBL's share of incremental category growth below ~40%.
- Another related-party or >₹500 cr acquisition.
